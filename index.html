Pehredaar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Pehredaar ("the app") is a caller ID and spam protection app for Android, developed by Rakesh Yadav. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Call log and contacts: The app reads your call log and contacts only to show recent calls, caller names and spam labels. This data is not uploaded to any server.
  • Block list, call history checks, settings, app lock PIN and saved names: Stored only on your device.
  • Messages you check: If you paste or share a message to the scam checker, it is analysed on your device. It is sent to an AI service only if you tap the AI check button (see below).

Data sent to our server

When you report a number as spam, or block a number (if the auto report setting is on), the app sends the following to our database (hosted on Supabase): the phone number, the category you selected (for example Spam or Scam), and a random anonymous device ID. The device ID is generated by the app and is not linked to your name, phone number, contacts or account. We use this to show other users how many people reported a number. We do not collect your name, your own phone number, your contacts or your call log. You can turn off auto report in Settings.

Abuse protection

To prevent fake reports, the server keeps a temporary log for up to 24 hours containing a hashed form of your network (IP) address, your anonymous device ID and the number you voted on. It is automatically deleted after 24 hours and is not used for any other purpose.

AI features

If you use AI features and add your own Google Gemini API key, the text you submit (such as a phone number or a message you want checked) is sent to Google's Gemini service to generate an answer. Your API key is stored only on your device. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show and measure ads. This happens in Google's systems, and Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's Settings, Google, Ads. Ads are shown only on the Home and Settings screens, never during a call.

Permissions

  • Phone / call log: to show recent calls, detect incoming calls and let you block spam.
  • Contacts: to show names of saved contacts.
  • Notifications and display over other apps: to warn you about suspicious incoming calls.
  • Microphone: only for optional voice features you choose to use.
  • Internet: for spam report counts, ads and optional AI features.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Reported numbers and categories are shown to other users only as an anonymous count. Ad data is handled by Google AdMob as described above.

Retention and deletion

Data stored on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To request removal of a number you reported, or if you have any question, email us at jhulan979@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
